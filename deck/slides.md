# Sample deck: know your number

A three-slide sample so the workspace runs on first open. Replace this file with your course deck
(a PDF, or a `slides.md` in this same shape) by copying it into this `deck/` folder.

## Slide 1: Who has the problem?
Pick the person before the product. Name one real person or business who has the problem today.
A market in pain with no money is a charity.
Notes: Ask for a name, not a category. "Roofers" is a category; "Sam's roofing crew" is a person.

## Slide 2: What does the problem cost them?
Put a number on the pain: hours lost, money missed, jobs that slip. Use their numbers, not a guess.
Notes: If they do not know the number, the next step is to go and ask the customer.

## Slide 3: Your floor price
Your floor is what it costs you to deliver: your hours times your rate, plus your tools.
Below the floor you are paying to work. Above it, price against what the problem costs them.
Notes: Work the floor out with the learner's own hours and rate. They pick the final price.
