# Walkthrough: my answers and decisions, slide by slide

One entry per slide, added after the learner answers. Shape of each entry:

## Slide N: <slide title>
- **Point:** what the slide teaches, two or three sentences
- **For my project:** what it means for my real work
- **Question:** the one question asked
- **My answer:** the learner's answer, in their words
- **Decided:** what they decided, in their words, or "nothing yet" (also when the slide asks for a
  figure, such as a price, that they have not picked)
- **Follow-up:** anything to check or do, or "none"

A skipped slide is one line: `## Slide N: <title>` then `- **Skipped**`.
